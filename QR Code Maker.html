Good to know

Does the QR expire? No. The link is stored inside the QR itself, so it works for as long as the link works.

Is it free and private? Yes. The QR is made on your phone. Nothing you type is sent or saved anywhere.

Link changed later? Then make a new QR. To avoid that, always use a link that never changes.
